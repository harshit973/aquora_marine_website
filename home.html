    The Aquora 4A Approach

    Align. Assess. Analyse. Advise.

    The Aquora 4A Approach turns a defined requirement into an independent evidence base, risk-ranked priorities and practical, decision-ready direction.

    One connected process

    From expectation to evidence. From evidence to direction.

    01 · Align

    Establish the decision or improvement the assignment must support.

    We establish the context before evidence is gathered, so the assignment is directed at a clear decision and agreed outcome.

    Outcome

    An agreed assignment brief.

    Key activities
    • Understand the operational, technical or commercial context
    • Define the decision or improvement to be supported
    • Agree criteria, scope and exclusions
    • Confirm information, access and responsibilities
    • Agree deliverables and programme
    • Propose and agree commercial terms
    Align
    Confirm context, scope, criteria, exclusions, information, access, responsibilities, deliverables, programme and commercial terms. Outcome: an agreed assignment brief.
    Assess
    Examine the vessel, systems, records, data and practices and test whether controls work in practice. Outcome: an independent, evidence-based assessment.
    Analyse
    Interpret evidence across safety, regulatory, operational and commercial dimensions. Outcome: prioritised findings with implications explained.
    Advise
    Translate analysis into conclusions, recommendations and proportionate next steps. Outcome: decision-ready professional direction.
